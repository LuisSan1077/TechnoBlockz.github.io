Techno
Blocks

Build a techno track, then play a 3D falling-block game locked to its beat. Every sound is synthesized live in your browser — no samples.

Headphones recommended. Volume starts at 80%.

Controls

← →Move left and right ↓Soft drop (+1 point a row) SpaceHard drop (+2 points a row) ↑ / XRotate clockwise ZRotate counter-clockwise P / EscPause TouchSwipe to move, tap to rotate, swipe down to hard drop, or hold the pad buttons under the board

The music plays the game

GravityPieces fall on the beat, so the tempo sets the speed. GrooveAct on a kick drum to raise the score multiplier. Clears1 line sweeps the filter, 2 fires a clap roll, 3 a riser, 4 the full drop — the room tears and glitches with it. LayersEvery line you clear adds an instrument to the track. Go too long without one and the newest layer drops out until you earn it back. DangerA high stack muffles the track and reddens the room until you dig out.
